A morning reflection that changes next week

8 minute read · Updated 29 October 2026Jotty field guide

Reflection only works when it ends in a decision. Jotty asks three questions about pages that actually happened, then turns your answers into one change for the week.

Why the questions are not a journal

A journal invites you to describe a mood. A coach asks about a moment, then asks what to do differently. Jotty writes the second kind. Each question is built from yesterday's log: the time you opened the page, the site, the page title, the task that was open, and the day-and-hour pattern from the last four weeks.

That means the question cannot be answered with "I was tired". It can be answered with "I clicked a recommendation while the problem set was open". The second answer is actionable.

The three shapes

1. Intention

"At 16:12 you were on YouTube, with gas stoichiometry still open. Was that a break you chose?" The answer changes the plan. A chosen break is not a problem. A drift is a doorway problem.

2. The moment before

"What was the last thing you did on the assignment before YouTube?" This is the question most people skip, and it is the one that produces a useful answer: stuck on problem 4, phone buzzed, opened the next tab by habit. The trigger is easier to change than the site.

3. Repair

"Tuesday at 16:00 is where this keeps happening. What should be different next time?" Four options, one of which becomes the plan: a soft block, a shorter session, the Focus Player, or an environment change.

Answer honestly, not impressively

The reflection is local. Nobody reads it but you. Still, people tend to pick the answer that makes them look disciplined, which turns the plan into fiction. If you drifted, choose "I drifted there". The plan that follows is milder than you expect: a delay screen at the hour where it repeats, not an intervention.

A useful test: if your answers could be pasted into any week of your term, they are too general. Name the trigger, the tab, the problem number.

From answers to a plan

What you answerWhat Jotty proposes
"I drifted there"A soft block at the weekday and hour where drift repeats.
"The sidebar pulled me in"The Focus Player for that subject, so there is no sidebar.
"I planned that break"Nothing. The break is left alone, and the plan focuses on the session end.
"I needed the video"Search inside the player instead of YouTube search.

What to do on a clean day

Jotty still asks one question: what made the day easier? The answer is worth keeping because it is the only part of the system that is already working. "I started before opening anything else" is a strategy. Write it down and repeat it.

How to review a week

Open the heatmap on Monday. Read the three weak windows, then read last week's plans. If the same window appears with the same plan three weeks in a row, the plan is wrong, not you. Change the intervention: shorter session, different chair, no phone, or a block that lasts the whole hour.
