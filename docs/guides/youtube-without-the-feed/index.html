Study on YouTube without the sidebar

6 minute read · Updated 29 October 2026Jotty field guide

YouTube is where the explanation lives and also where the study session ends. You do not have to choose between the two; you have to remove the part that is designed to keep you.

The sidebar is the product

A video page is not just a video. It is a video plus a column of next steps, each one ranked to be slightly more interesting than what you searched for. When you opened the page, you had a question. By the time the video ends, the question is gone and the column is in charge.

That is why "just be disciplined" fails. The recommendation is not a temptation you chose to accept; it is the default surface of the site. Removing it is a design decision, not a character trait.

What the Focus Player changes

  • Search happens in Jotty. You type the task, not a vague curiosity. Results show title, channel, duration and views.
  • Your channels come first. A per-subject allowlist moves channels you already trust to the top.
  • Short clips are hidden. Anything under ninety seconds is not a lesson, and it is not shown.
  • Playback is embedded. The video plays from youtube-nocookie.com with related videos limited to the same channel and annotation cards off.
  • There is no queue. When the video ends, the player suggests nothing. Closing the tab is the next action.

What it does not do

It does not block YouTube. You can still open youtube.com, and during a session Jotty will judge the page and nudge you if it is off task. If you want friction, add YouTube to the site list in Settings and turn on the delay. Strict mode removes the override for the length of a session.

It also does not download videos, strip ads, or bypass YouTube's player. The embed is the official one. Ads may still appear depending on your account and region. Jotty shows no ads of its own.

The honest limits of an embed

YouTube controls the player. Parameters such as rel=0 reduce related videos but do not remove every surface, and they can change. Treat the clean player as a strong default, not a guarantee. The stronger guarantee is structural: Jotty never shows you a results page built to keep you scrolling.

Setting it up

  1. Open Settings → Focus Player and add a YouTube Data API key. The key guide takes six steps.
  2. Edit the trusted channel list for your subject. The defaults are real channels; remove the ones you do not use.
  3. Start a focus session with a task, then open the player from the popup.
  4. Search the task. If a result is good but the channel is new, press "Trust this channel" once.
Each search costs about 101 of the 10,000 free daily units. Jotty shows the running total in Settings, so you can see the cost instead of guessing.

If you do not want an API key

The rest of Jotty works without one. The player will show your channel list and explain that search is off. That is a deliberate trade: no key, no scraping, no pretending.
