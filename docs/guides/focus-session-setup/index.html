Write a task line the judge can use

5 minute read · Updated 29 October 2026Jotty field guide

The task line is the whole input. A vague task produces vague judgements; a specific one lets Jotty tell the subject apart from the assignment.

What Jotty does with the line

The line is reduced to content words, expanded with the subject's vocabulary, and compared with the page title, address and channel. The subject field selects the vocabulary. That is why both fields exist, and why "study" is not a task.

The shape that works

Topic + scope + the thing you will produce.

Instead ofWrite
ChemistryGas stoichiometry, chapter 9 problems 4 to 12
History essayCold War essay: Cuban Missile Crisis, argument outline
CodeBuild the SQL normalisation worksheet, 3NF examples
Biology revisionKrebs cycle and electron transport chain, exam questions
ReadingRead Frankenstein chapters 4 to 6 and take notes on the creature

Why the scope helps

A video called "Ideal gas law and stoichiometry" matches the words gas and stoichiometry. It is on task. A video called "The chemistry of Teflon" contains none of the task words, only the subject, so it is classified as related but off task. That distinction is the product. If the task were just "chemistry", both would pass and the nudge would never appear.

Choose the subject honestly

The subject powers the topic clusters. If you are writing a history essay, choose History so that a video about the Cold War is recognised as the subject even when it does not match the essay's exact words. If you leave the subject on Chemistry while writing history, adjacent material will be called off task.

The deadline is not decoration

The deadline appears in the session and in the morning reflection, where it supplies context for the question. Jotty does not schedule anything around it and does not send escalating reminders. If the deadline has passed, the session still runs; the label simply says so.

Session length

Fifty minutes is the default because it fits a class period. Twenty-five is better for a first session, especially if your habit is abandoning long blocks. The length affects the clock and the end-of-session notification, not the judgement.

When the judge is wrong

  • Add the domain to the allowlist if it is a reference site you use constantly.
  • Add the channel to your trusted list if the videos are consistently relevant.
  • Press "This helps" on the nudge. The feedback stays local, but it is the honest signal that the rule needs work.
  • Make the task line narrower. Most false nudges come from tasks that name a subject instead of an assignment.
