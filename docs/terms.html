2. What Jotty is

Jotty is a local browser extension that compares page metadata with a task you type, shows a nudge or a delay screen, keeps a local habit log, and generates reflective questions. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no hosting service for your data and no account to maintain.

5. No guaranteed judgement

You can snooze it, ignore it, or turn the product off. Jotty does not block access at the network level and cannot prevent you from visiting a site.
